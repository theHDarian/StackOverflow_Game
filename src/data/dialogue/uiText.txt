UI ControlsGuide
Controls:
[WASD] to move
[SPACE/RMB] to dash
[LMB] to shoot
[E] to progress dialogue
[C] to call the scientist for help
[ESC] to pause
[R] to return to title
[L] to leave game
[=/-] to increase and decrease volume
[V] to toggle collider visuals
[F] to toggle FPS counter
