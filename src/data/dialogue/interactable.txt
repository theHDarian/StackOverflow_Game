ITEM CallScientist 0 0
When you get hit by enemies, you will get the corresponding effects pushed onto your stack.
These bullets will modify you stats, with the exception of INERT and KEYS effects. Keys are useful for unlocking doors, but inert bullets are just plain useless!
Whenever you collide with an enemy, you'll only get an inert bullet, so try not to run into them!

ITEM CallScientist 1 0
There are 5 different room types: REST rooms, TREASURE rooms, EVENT rooms, ENEMY rooms, and BOSS rooms.
Rest rooms have a POP console in them that allow you to pop all items on your stack, and then an oppurtunity to collect any effects you want to keep.
Treasure rooms have special bullet effects in them delivered via PUSH and FIGHT consoles. Though the rewards are great for fight consoles, you do have to fight for them!
Event rooms are mysterious rooms with special NPC reactions that you can only encounter once.
Enemy rooms are filled with various rogue robots.
And finally, boss rooms have especially dangerous rogue robots. Unfortunately, once you've entered their domain, you've no choice but to fight them!

ITEM CallScientist 2 0
"LIGHTNING bullets" are packets of concentrated electricity that leak from severely damaged robots, and thus, do not add effects to your stack or take up stack space.
However,if you make contact with these packets, your systems will experience a shock, causing the order of data on your stack will be rearranged.
BLUE lightning shifts all your effects to the right by one, and YELLOW shuffles your stack.
When rogue robots are nearing death, they tend to spit out more of these lightning bullets.
The order of effects in your stack could be important when it comes to using keys, which will remove all effects following the key once you use them!

ITEM CallScientist 3 0
KEYS are special bullets that are occasionally fired by rogue robots. They are used to unlock locked rooms.
These rooms tend to have stronger enemies or even better rewards!
